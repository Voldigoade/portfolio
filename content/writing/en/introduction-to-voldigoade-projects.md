# Introduction to Voldigoade Projects and Research

Welcome to the technical publications space for Voldigoade. This space hosts engineering notes, systems architecture write-ups, and independent research focused on security, privacy, and software robustness.

## Primary Technical Focus Areas

1. **Digital Footprint & Inspection**: Fast, reliable Rust CLI utilities such as [spoor](https://github.com/Voldigoade/spoor), engineered to audit public GitHub footprints non-intrusively.
2. **Threat Modeling & Secure Messaging**: Rigorous comparative research into modern secure communication protocols (Signal, SimpleX, Session, Olvid, Threema, Briar) under the [recherche-messageries](https://github.com/Voldigoade/recherche-messageries) initiative.
3. **Developer Tools & Environments**: Investigating IDE mechanics and high-efficiency workflows.

## Principles

Every project prioritizes:
- Verifiable source code and rigorous boundaries.
- Minimizing attack surfaces and eliminating intrusive telemetry.
- Direct, resilient, and performant user experiences.

Technical feedback and discussions are welcome directly on the respective GitHub repositories.
